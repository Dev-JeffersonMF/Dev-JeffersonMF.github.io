## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a gallery wall, not a grid of cards. Every delivered system is a mounted work under focused light with a small plaque of facts; the newest work, Nexo, occupies the first wall. The category-default hero-with-buttons and equal-card row are refused.

OWN-WORLD: Deep warm-charcoal gallery ground, off-white mounts, one restrained brass accent used only for the lit work and the primary action. Each work sits inside a thin picture frame with a real photographic print and a typed plaque. Vertical rhythm comes from wall spacing; type is a self-hosted grotesque for plaques and a controlled display voice for the wall title.

STORY: The visitor walks in, sees Nexo lit first, understands this person ships real systems, continues along the wall project by project, and leaves through a plainly visible contact plaque.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height wall. Left: the name and one sentence of what he does, set directly on the wall without an eyebrow. Right and centre: the Nexo work, framed, larger than the rest, with its icon reduced to fit and a plaque carrying the public link named “Nexo”. The primary contact action sits on the plaque area, not in a detached header button.

FORM: Gallery wall, candidate 4 of the grounded list. Raised by the glazier partition's rule that one saturated field carries attention, and by the detector display's rule that every label is specific and measurable.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
